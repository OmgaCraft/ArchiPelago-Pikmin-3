# Pikmin 3

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export
a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every fruit you juice sends a check to the multiworld. Fruits no longer give juice when they are juiced: your juice
(your survival time) arrives as items instead, named after the fruits of Pikmin 3. Discovering Onions, obtaining key
items and upgrades, rescuing Louie, reading GamePad notes (tips, Olimar's notes, Secret Memos) and reaching Pikmin
population milestones can also be checks.

With the experimental **Progressive Pikmin Limit** option, the number of Pikmin you can have on the field starts low
and grows with each "Progressive Pikmin Limit" item you receive, up to the usual 100.

## What is the goal?

Depending on your options: defeat the final boss **and** juice a given number of fruits (default), juice a given
number of fruits, or reach a given Pikmin population.

With the experimental **Progressive Zones** option, you start with Tropical Wilds and Garden of Hope, and each
"Progressive Zone" item opens the next area on the ship's map (Distant Tundra, Twilight River, then Formidable Oak,
home of the final boss). With the experimental **Progressive Pikmin** option (off by default), four items unlock
Rock, Yellow, Winged and Blue Pikmin.

## Which version of the game is supported?

Version 1 (disc, no update) of **Pikmin 3 (Europe)** on Wii U, running on the **Cemu** emulator (2.6 or newer) on
Windows. Other versions are not supported yet.

## What does another world's item look like in Pikmin 3?

Nothing changes in the game itself: the client shows received and sent items in its window.
